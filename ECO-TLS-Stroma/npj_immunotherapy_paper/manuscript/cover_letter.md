Dear Editors,

We submit our Original Research Article for consideration in your Collection. Moving beyond single-analyte biomarkers, we formalize the tumor-ecosystem balance between TLS organization and stromal exclusion as a transparent two-program score (ECO = TLS − STROMA, literature-fixed gene lists, no model fitting) and validate it by strict cohort-to-cohort analysis in **9 public cohorts (≈1,830 tumors)**: TCGA melanoma/lung/bladder for prognosis and **six independent ICI cohorts across 3 cancers** for response and survival.

Key findings: (1) ECO is higher in responders in all six ICI cohorts (AUC 0.60–0.78; pooled AUC 0.62, n = 439), beating TLS-alone in 4/6 and PD-L1 in 6/6; (2) ECO stratifies OS in all three TCGA cohorts (HRs 0.53–0.64) and in atezolizumab-treated IMvigor210 (HR 0.69); (3) ecosystem subtyping identifies stromal-high tumors as the least responsive state (pooled 19.2% vs 28.2%), prioritizing stromal remodeling as a targetable resistance program; (4) ECO is largely orthogonal to TMB (ρ = 0.26). All analyses use only public data with fully released code; no new human data were generated.

The work fits the Collection’s scope squarely — *mechanisms of resistance / tumor–immune–stromal crosstalk; AI/computational and multimodal biomarkers; translational stratification* — and offers an immediately computable, interpretable readout for trial stratification and stromal-directed combination hypotheses.

The manuscript is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approve submission. We declare no competing interests.
